# HariOm SmartMart

## Introduction

HariOm SmartMart is a web-based Digital Grocery Ordering, Supplier Collaboration, and Inventory Management System. The main objective of this project is to provide a complete online platform where customers can buy groceries, wholesalers can supply products, and administrators can efficiently manage inventory and operations.

This system replaces traditional manual grocery store management with a modern digital solution. Customers can browse products, place orders, track deliveries, and make secure online payments. Administrators can manage products, monitor stock levels, handle supplier approvals, and control product expiry management.

HariOm SmartMart provides a centralized platform where all major business activities such as product management, order processing, payment handling, supplier collaboration, notifications, and communication are integrated into a single system.

# System Overview

HariOm SmartMart works as a multi-user online grocery platform that manages three types of users:

- Customers
- Admin
- Wholesalers

### Workflow of the System

- Customers register and log in securely.
- Users browse grocery products by categories.
- Products can be added to cart and wishlist.
- Customers place orders and complete secure online payment.
- Orders are stored and managed in the database.
- Admin manages products, categories, orders, coupons, and payments.
- Wholesalers register and upload supply offers.
- Admin approves or rejects wholesalers.
- Admin reviews supplier offers and approved offers are added to the shop inventory.
- The system automatically checks product expiry dates and disables expired products.
- Notifications are generated for orders, chats, and supplier activities.

This integrated workflow ensures smooth, organized, and efficient grocery business management.
